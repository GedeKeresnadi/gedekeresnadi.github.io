Overview Problem Process Results Learnings

01 — OVERVIEW

What is this project?

This project started from a recurring pain point in the HR team — nobody could clearly explain why attrition was spiking in certain departments without pulling hours of manual reports.

I built a centralized dashboard that consolidates headcount, resignation records, and exit interview data into a single view, giving HR managers real-time visibility into which teams are at risk and why.

→ The dashboard identified that 68% of voluntary resignations in Q3 came from employees with less than 18 months tenure — leading to a revised onboarding program.

02 — PROBLEM

What problem did I solve?

The HR team was relying on monthly Excel exports with no drill-down capability. Leadership questions like "which region has the highest attrition?" took days to answer manually.

The core challenge was connecting three separate data sources — HRIS records, exit interviews, and payroll — that had never been joined before.

03 — PROCESS

How I built it

01

Data audit & source mapping

Identified all relevant tables in BigQuery and documented field definitions, nulls, and join keys across 3 source systems.

02

SQL modeling

Wrote layered CTEs to clean, standardize, and join the data into a single flat table ready for visualization.

03

Dashboard design in Looker Studio

Built an interactive dashboard with department-level filters, tenure cohort breakdowns, and a rolling 12-month attrition rate trend.

04

Stakeholder review & iteration

Presented to HR leadership, gathered feedback on missing metrics, and shipped two rounds of iteration based on real usage.

05 — LEARNINGS

What I'd do differently

I underestimated how much time stakeholder alignment would take early on. Defining the key questions upfront — before touching any data — would have saved at least two rounds of rework.

I'd also build automated data freshness checks from the start, rather than adding them after users reported stale numbers.
